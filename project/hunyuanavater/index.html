Abstract

Audio-driven human animation has made significant progress in recent years. However, existing state-of-the-art methods still face challenges in handling multi-speaker audio-driven scenarios, non-frontal viewpoints, rendering facial emotions of portraits, and generating immersive, realistic backgrounds. In this paper, we propose HunyuanVideo-Avatar, a model based on the MM-DiT architecture, which is capable of generating portrait animation videos featuring multi-person conversations, high consistency, high dynamism, and audio-aligned emotions. To address the limitation of current methods in handling audio-driven animation for multiple characters, we design a Face Audio Adapter (FAA) for the face region, enabling separate audio-driven animation for each character. This module is primarily composed of cross-attention mechanisms. In addition, we introduce a character image injection module, which ensures character consistency while maintaining dynamic motion. We also design an Audio Mood Module (AMM) to align the emotions of video characters with those expressed in the audio, using reference images for audio emotion. Compared with existing audio-driven human animation methods, HunyuanVideo-Avatar not only generates more realistic videos, but also maintains high dynamism while preserving character consistency. Experimental results on both benchmark datasets and our newly proposed wild dataset demonstrate that our method achieves significant improvements over existing approaches in generating realistic portraits with diverse viewpoints in dynamic and immersive scenes.
